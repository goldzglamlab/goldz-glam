A little about Goldz Glam

Goldz Glam Laboratory grew from my love of doing hair. I started styling hair on the side, and word of mouth, referrals and people asking about my looks encouraged me to take it further.

Now I'm building a home-based business in Saint Lucia, focusing on wig installations, sew-in weaves and styling. I want every client to feel good about their hair and enjoy their appointment.
